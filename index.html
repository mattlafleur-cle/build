2Attend

Bring your value to the room.
Carry value away.

Owners spend most of the week alone in their own head. A BUILD room is a table of people who have been there. You hear the room, and the room hears you. Every room runs the same way.

First

Around the table

  1. Your name
  2. The business you own
  3. The main thing you sell
  4. What you are looking for today
Then

One real question

One question goes in the middle of the table, something like “If you disappeared for thirty days, what would break first?” Then the facilitator asks who else has lived it and how they got out. Owners learn from each other, not from a lecture.

Last

The nugget round

Everyone speaks before anyone leaves. Share one thing you heard that you will try, or one thing you have been through and can help with. The facilitator sends those introductions within a day.

The BUILD 52 Framework

Every business is somewhere on one journey.

There is no shame in where you stand. A Startup is not a failed Scale Up, any more than a seed is a failed tree. The goal is to know your stage and work on what it needs.

1

Startup

Build the foundation and get traction.

The owner does everything, and for now that is correct. Prove that strangers will pay a fair price for what you do, again and again.

Graduates whenDemand is steady, your price holds, and the biggest thing slowing the company down is you.

2

Grow Up

Strengthen systems, team, and offers.

The business gets organized. First hires land, cash finds a floor, and the machine gets built piece by piece.

Graduates whenYour best crew lead could run daily operations for two weeks without calling you.

3

Scale Up

Increase impact, revenue, and reach.

A leadership team runs the company and you lead the leaders. The goal gets big enough that the old way obviously cannot reach it.

Graduates whenThe company grows while you take real time away, and nobody has to cover for you.

4

Hand Off

A business that runs without you.

The owner is optional and the exit is a choice, not a rescue. Sell it, keep it as an asset that pays you, or hand it down.

All three are winsThe trap was never the business. The trap was having no choice.

Written for the owner who still knows where the shovels are. Every tool in the same six lines: what it is, when to run it, how to run it, what you end up holding, what to watch out for, and where it came from.

The Five A's
  • 1Awareness Gathering. Get the truth on the table.
  • 2Analyze. Sort what matters from what is noise.
  • 3Alignment. Put it on a timeline everyone can see.
  • 4Action. One owner, one date, one definition of done.
  • 5Accountability. Keep score in the open.
